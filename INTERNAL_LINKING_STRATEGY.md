## Best Practices for Linking

### Anchor Text Strategy
- Use descriptive anchor text: "real estate CRM software", "CRM features", "lead management system"
- Avoid exact-match keywords everywhere - vary the language naturally
- Contextual links should flow organically from the content
- Do not over-link - focus on meaningful connections

### Link Placement Guidelines
1. **Introduction**: Brief mention in opening paragraph for high-priority articles
2. **Key Sections**: Near feature descriptions or benefits discussions
3. **Conclusion**: Final recommendation or summary with CRM link
4. **End of Article**: Prominent CTA using BlogCtaImproved component

### Quality Standards
- All links must be relevant to the content
- Links should not interrupt the reading flow
- Use canonical URLs consistently 
- Maintain descriptive anchor text
- Ensure all internal links work properly

## Implementation Notes

### For Future Updates:
1. When updating blog content, add relevant CRM links in appropriate sections
