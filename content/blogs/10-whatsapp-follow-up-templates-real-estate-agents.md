In real estate, speed and consistency are everything. Over 90% of homebuyer communications occur over WhatsApp, yet over 70% of property leads drop off due to poor or repetitive follow-ups like *"Sir, any update?"*

To convert raw enquiries into signed bookings, your follow-up sequence must deliver value, establish trust, and lower buying friction. 

Here are **10 proven WhatsApp follow-up templates** designed for real estate agents, brokers, and sales teams to boost site visits and close more deals.

---

### Template 1: Instant Lead Response (0–5 Minutes)
* **Goal:** Acknowledge enquiry instantly before the lead contacts a competitor.
* **Trigger:** Automatically upon lead capture from FB/Meta Ads, 99acres, or MagicBricks.

> **WhatsApp Copy:**  
> Hi {{1}} 👋  
> Thank you for inquiring about **{{2}}** in {{3}}!  
> 
> Here is the digital brochure and master layout plan 📎.  
> 
> We have 2 BHK & 3 BHK configurations starting at ₹{{4}}. Which layout fits your requirement better—2 BHK or 3 BHK?  
> 
> Best regards,  
> {{5}} | {{6}}

---

### Template 2: Price & Value Hook (2 Hours Later)
* **Goal:** Hook buyer interest with specific financial perks.

> **WhatsApp Copy:**  
> Hi {{1}}, quick update on **{{2}}** 👇  
> 
> The current pre-launch pricing offers savings of up to **₹{{3}}** for bookings made this month.  
> 
> Would you like me to share the exact cost sheet breakdown including floor-rise charges?

---

### Template 3: The "Yes-Yes" Site Visit Invitation (Day 1)
* **Goal:** Schedule an in-person site visit using choice architecture.

> **WhatsApp Copy:**  
> Hi {{1}}! We are hosting guided walkthroughs of our sample flat at **{{2}}** this weekend.  
> 
> Pickup and drop options are available for you and your family.  
> 
> Which slot works better for you—**Saturday Morning (11 AM)** or **Sunday Afternoon (3 PM)**?

---

### Template 4: Post-Site Visit Follow-Up (Same Evening)
* **Goal:** Strike while interest is hot right after a site visit.

> **WhatsApp Copy:**  
> Hi {{1}}, it was great meeting you and your family at **{{2}}** today!  
> 
> I noticed you loved the {{3}}. Attached is the exact payment plan for **Unit #{{4}}** we discussed.  
> 
> Let me know if you have any questions regarding the bank loan process or booking terms.

---

### Template 5: Addressing Specific Objections (Day 3)
* **Goal:** Resolve hesitations regarding price, possession timeline, or builder trust.

> **WhatsApp Copy:**  
> Hi {{1}} 👋  
> 
> I remember you mentioned concerns about {{2}} during our chat.  
> 
> I’ve compiled the official RERA registration document and bank approvals list for **{{3}}** 📄. Should I send the PDF over?

---

### Template 6: Social Proof & Construction Progress (Day 7)
* **Goal:** Build trust with video proof and community momentum.

> **WhatsApp Copy:**  
> Hi {{1}}! Here is a short video of this week's construction progress on {{2}} 🏗️.  
> 
> Over {{3}} families booked their units here last month! Let me know if you'd like to check updated unit availability.

---

### Template 7: Soft Urgency / Inventory Shift (Day 10)
* **Goal:** Motivate passive leads without sounding aggressive.

> **WhatsApp Copy:**  
> Hi {{1}}, just a heads-up 🏷️  
> 
> The **{{2}}** unit you liked had two enquiries today.  
> 
> If you are still evaluating, I can hold this unit for 24 hours without a booking token. Should I request the developer for a temporary hold?

---

### Template 8: Re-Engaging "Ghosted" Leads (Day 20)
* **Goal:** Bring cold or silent leads back into the active pipeline.

> **WhatsApp Copy:**  
> Hi {{1}} 👋  
> 
> I know you've been busy. Quick question: Are you still looking for a home in {{2}}, or have you put your property search on hold for now?  
> 
> Either way, let me know so I don't flood your WhatsApp with updates!

---

### Template 9: Festive or Exclusive Offer Trigger (Day 30)
* **Goal:** Re-ignite dead leads with a fresh financial incentive.

> **WhatsApp Copy:**  
> Hi {{1}} ✨  
> 
> Limited-time benefit on **{{2}}**: The developer is offering a **{{3}}** for bookings completed before {{4}}.  
> 
> Should I check if your preferred unit qualifies for this offer?

---

### Template 10: Post-Booking Referral & Engagement
* **Goal:** Drive organic referrals from existing buyers.

> **WhatsApp Copy:**  
> Hi {{1}}, congratulations again on securing your home at **{{2}}** 🎉!  
> 
> Our developer is running a **Referral Bonus Program**—if any friend or colleague books a unit through your reference, you both receive a ₹{{3}} cashback voucher.  
> 
> Feel free to pass along my contact details!
