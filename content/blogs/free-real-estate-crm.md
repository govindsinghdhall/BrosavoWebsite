# Free Real Estate CRM: What Should You Look For?

A **free real estate CRM** can be a useful starting point for agents, property consultants and small real estate teams that want to organize leads, customers and follow-ups without immediately committing to a large software expense.

However, "free" does not always mean unlimited.

Free CRM plans can have restrictions on the number of users, leads, storage, features, reporting capabilities or integrations.

This guide explains what to look for in free real estate CRM software and how to determine whether a free plan is actually suitable for your real estate business.

Brosavo is a technology company building software for real estate businesses, including real estate CRM and lead management workflows.

> **Important:** Brosavo should not be described as a permanently free CRM unless a free plan is currently offered. If Brosavo is offering a free trial, demo or another introductory option, evaluate that offer based on its current terms and pricing.

---

## What Is a Free Real Estate CRM?

A free real estate CRM is customer relationship management software that can be used at no cost, either through a permanent free plan or a limited free-access model such as a free trial.

A real estate CRM can help businesses organize:

- Leads
- Customer information
- Property enquiries
- Follow-ups
- Sales activities
- Sales pipeline
- Property-related information
- Team activity

The important distinction is between a **permanently free CRM plan** and a **time-limited free trial**.

Before choosing a platform, always check exactly what "free" includes.

---

## Is There a Free Real Estate CRM?

Yes, there are CRM products that offer free plans or free trials, but the limits can vary significantly.

Some free plans may limit:

- Number of users
- Number of contacts
- Number of leads
- Storage
- Reports
- Automation
- Integrations
- Customer support
- Property records

For an individual real estate agent, a limited free plan may be enough to get started.

For a brokerage, developer or growing sales team, the limitations may become important very quickly.

---

## What Should a Free Real Estate CRM Include?

A useful free real estate CRM should provide more than just a contact list.

At minimum, real estate users should evaluate whether the CRM can help them manage:

### Lead Management

Can you record and organize property enquiries?

### Customer Management

Can you maintain customer information and interactions?

### Follow-Ups

Can you keep track of conversations and future follow-up activities?

### Sales Pipeline

Can you understand where each opportunity is in the sales process?

### Property Information

Can you associate customer requirements with relevant properties or projects?

### Reporting

Can you understand your sales activity and pipeline?

### Upgrade Options

Can you move to a paid plan when your business grows?

These questions are often more important than simply asking whether a CRM is free.

# Why Do Real Estate Agents Look for Free CRM Software?

Real estate agents often start with spreadsheets, phone contacts, messaging applications or simple notes.

That can work when the number of leads is small.

As the number of enquiries increases, however, it becomes harder to remember:

- Who contacted you?
- Which property were they interested in?
- When should you follow up?
- What did they previously ask?
- Did they visit the property?
- Are they still interested?
- Which leads are likely to convert?

A CRM gives the agent a centralized system for managing these relationships.

For agents who are just starting to use CRM software, a free plan or trial can provide an opportunity to understand whether a CRM fits their workflow.

# Can I Manage Property Leads With a Free CRM?

Yes, a free CRM can be used to manage property leads if the plan includes the required lead-management capabilities.

A basic workflow could look like:

**New Lead → Qualification → Property Discussion → Follow-Up → Site Visit → Negotiation → Closing**

The exact workflow depends on the CRM.

Before choosing a free CRM, check whether the plan actually supports the number of leads and activities your business handles.

# What Are the Main Limitations of Free Real Estate CRM Software?

Free software usually has some form of limitation.

These limitations are not necessarily a problem.

They simply need to match the size and requirements of your business.

## 1. Lead Limits

Some free plans restrict the number of contacts or leads that can be stored.

An individual agent with 50 active leads may have very different requirements from a brokerage handling thousands of enquiries.

---

## 2. User Limits

A free plan may be designed for a single user or a very small team.

If multiple agents need access, check the number of users included.

---

## 3. Storage Limits

Some CRM platforms restrict the amount of data or file storage available on a free plan.

This becomes relevant if your team stores substantial property information or documents.

---

## 4. Reporting Limits

Advanced dashboards and reports may only be available on paid plans.

If sales management is important to your business, check exactly what reporting capabilities are included.

---

## 5. Integration Limits

A free CRM may provide limited integrations.

Before choosing a platform, determine whether the systems you depend on can be connected to the CRM.

Never assume an integration exists simply because the CRM advertises itself as "free."

---

## 6. Support Limits

Free plans may provide limited customer support compared with paid subscriptions.

For a business-critical CRM, support can become an important consideration.

# Free CRM vs Paid Real Estate CRM

The right choice depends on the size and complexity of your business.

### Free CRM

A free CRM may be suitable when:

- You are an individual agent
- You have a small number of leads
- You are experimenting with CRM software
- Your workflow is relatively simple
- You do not need advanced functionality

### Paid CRM

A paid CRM may become more appropriate when:

- Your lead volume increases
- Multiple agents need access
- You need more structured sales workflows
- You require more reporting
- Your property inventory grows
- You need more storage
- Your team requires additional support

The goal should not be to stay on a free plan forever.

The goal should be to choose software that remains useful as your business grows.
