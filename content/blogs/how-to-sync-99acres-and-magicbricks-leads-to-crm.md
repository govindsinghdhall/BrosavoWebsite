How to Sync 99acres and MagicBricks Leads to Your CRM in 2026

Real estate businesses often generate enquiries from multiple property portals, including 99acres and MagicBricks. The challenge starts after the enquiry arrives.

If leads remain separated across different portal dashboards, email notifications, spreadsheets, and messaging apps, sales teams can struggle to maintain a clear view of who has been contacted, who needs a follow-up, and which properties each customer is interested in.

Connecting property portal leads to a CRM can create a more organized workflow:

Portal enquiry → CRM lead → Assignment → Qualification → Follow-up → Property discussion → Site visit → Negotiation → Closing

The exact integration method depends on the portal account, available lead-delivery options, and CRM capabilities.

Key Takeaways

Centralizing 99acres and MagicBricks enquiries can reduce manual lead entry and improve visibility.

Direct integrations may be available depending on the portal account and CRM.

Email-based lead capture can be an alternative when direct API or webhook access is unavailable.

CSV import remains a practical option for teams that cannot use real-time lead delivery.

Every lead should retain its original source so teams can measure portal performance.

Lead ownership should be clearly assigned after a lead enters the CRM.

A CRM should track the lead from enquiry through qualification, follow-up, property discussion, site visit, negotiation, and closing where applicable.

Before implementing a direct portal integration, confirm what lead-access and integration options are available for your specific portal account.

What Does It Mean to Sync 99acres and MagicBricks Leads With a CRM?

Syncing 99acres and MagicBricks leads with a CRM means transferring property enquiries from those platforms into a centralized customer and sales management system.

Instead of keeping the enquiry inside a separate portal dashboard, the CRM can become the place where your team records the customer, tracks the property enquiry, assigns ownership, schedules follow-ups, and manages the sales pipeline.

The goal is not simply to move data. The goal is to make every enquiry a trackable sales opportunity.

How Do 99acres Leads Reach a CRM?

The correct method depends on the lead-delivery options available through your 99acres account and the capabilities of your CRM.

Direct CRM Integration

When supported, the portal can deliver lead information directly to the CRM instead of requiring a salesperson to manually copy the enquiry.

99acres → Integration → CRM → Sales Team

The exact setup can involve credentials, an approved endpoint, webhook configuration, or another lead-delivery mechanism. Confirm the integration options available for your specific portal account before implementing it.

How Do MagicBricks Leads Reach a CRM?

MagicBricks provides a response-management system where property responses can be viewed from the account dashboard and documents options for downloading response information.

Possible workflows depend on the account and CRM:

MagicBricks → Approved lead delivery → CRM

or:

MagicBricks → Export → CRM import

Avoid assuming that every MagicBricks account has identical integration capabilities.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I sync 99acres leads to a CRM?

The available method depends on your 99acres account and the CRM. Possible approaches include a supported direct lead-delivery integration, an approved email-based workflow, or CSV export and CRM import where direct synchronization is unavailable.

How do I sync MagicBricks leads to a CRM?

The exact method depends on the MagicBricks account and CRM. MagicBricks provides response-management and download functionality, so CSV-based import can be an option when direct integration is unavailable.

Can I connect both 99acres and MagicBricks to the same CRM?

Yes, provided the CRM supports the required lead-intake methods for both sources. The goal is to preserve the original source while bringing enquiries into one sales workflow.

Can portal leads automatically be assigned to sales agents?

They can be automatically assigned only when the CRM and lead-delivery setup supports assignment rules. Otherwise, teams can assign leads manually after they enter the CRM.

Should I import old 99acres and MagicBricks leads into my CRM?

Importing historical leads can be useful if those customers are still relevant to your business. Clean the data first and avoid creating duplicate customer records.

Is CSV import better than direct integration?

Not necessarily. Direct integration can reduce manual work when it is officially supported and properly configured. CSV import is useful as a fallback or for historical data.

Why should I track the portal source?

Source tracking lets you understand which channels generate enquiries and which sources produce qualified opportunities, site visits, or sales.

Can a CRM manage leads from multiple property portals?

Yes. A CRM can centralize leads from multiple sources when those sources can be connected through supported integrations or import workflows.

What should happen after a portal lead enters the CRM?

The lead should normally be assigned, qualified, followed up, connected with relevant property information, and moved through the sales pipeline according to the customer's progress.

Does Brosavo automatically integrate with 99acres and MagicBricks?

Do not assume this without confirming the current Brosavo integration configuration for your account. Portal synchronization depends on the supported lead-intake method available from the portal and implemented by the CRM.
