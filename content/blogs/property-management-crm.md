# Property Management CRM for Real Estate Teams

Brosavo provides a property management CRM designed to help real estate businesses organize property information, inventory, enquiries, customers and sales workflows in one system.

Unlike rental-focused property management platforms, Brosavo's property CRM is positioned around **property sales, inventory organization, lead management and customer workflows**.

Whether you are a real estate agent, broker, developer or property sales team, the goal is simple: keep your property information and customer activity organized so your team can manage enquiries and sales more effectively.

---

## What Is a Property Management CRM?

A property management CRM is software that helps real estate businesses organize property information together with customer, lead and sales activities.

For sales-focused real estate businesses, this can include property inventory, property details, enquiries, customer information, follow-ups, sales pipeline activity and related workflows.

Brosavo connects property-related information with the broader real estate CRM workflow, helping teams manage both the **property side** and the **customer side** of the sales process.

### Property management does not always mean rental management

The term "property management" can refer to different types of real estate operations.

Some platforms focus primarily on:

- Rent collection
- Tenant management
- Lease management
- Maintenance requests
- Tenant portals

Brosavo's property CRM positioning is different.

The focus is on **property sales and customer workflows**, including:

- Property inventory
- Property information
- Property enquiries
- Lead management
- Customer management
- Follow-ups
- Sales pipeline activity
- Property-related sales workflows

This makes Brosavo relevant to real estate businesses that need to organize properties alongside the sales process.

---

## Why Do Real Estate Businesses Need Property Management CRM Software?

Real estate businesses often manage large amounts of property information while simultaneously handling enquiries, customers and sales activities.

Without a centralized system, information can become spread across spreadsheets, documents, messaging applications and individual team members.

A property-focused CRM can help bring these workflows together.

For example, a real estate team may need to know:

- Which properties are currently available?
- Which customer is interested in a particular property?
- Which leads are being followed up?
- What stage is each enquiry in?
- Which properties have generated the most interest?
- Which customers require another follow-up?
- Which sales activities are currently in progress?

Brosavo is designed to connect these property and customer workflows within a real estate CRM environment.

## How Does Property Inventory Connect With Customer Enquiries?

A property CRM becomes more useful when property information and customer information can be considered together.

For example:

**Customer enquiry → Requirement → Property matching → Follow-up → Site visit → Sales process**

This allows the sales team to think about the customer's requirement in relation to the properties they may be interested in.

The exact workflow can vary depending on how a real estate business operates.

## Property Enquiry Management

Property enquiries are often the starting point of a real estate sales process.

An enquiry may come from a prospective buyer who is interested in:

- A specific property
- A project
- A location
- A property type
- A particular configuration
- A specific price range

A property CRM can help the sales team maintain the relationship between the enquiry, the customer and the relevant property information.

## Property Lead Matching

A property enquiry is more useful when the sales team can connect it with relevant property information.

A typical sales workflow can look like:

**Lead → Qualification → Requirement understanding → Property matching → Follow-up → Site visit → Negotiation → Closing**

Not every business will use every stage, but the underlying idea remains the same: connect customer requirements with the appropriate property and sales activity.
