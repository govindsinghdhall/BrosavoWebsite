## What Is a Real Estate CRM App?

A **real estate CRM app** is software designed to help real estate professionals manage leads, customers, property-related information, follow-ups and sales activities from a centralized system.

Unlike a general-purpose CRM, a real estate CRM is designed around the way property businesses work.

A typical real estate workflow may look like:

**Lead → Qualification → Property Requirement → Follow-Up → Property Discussion → Site Visit → Negotiation → Closing**

The exact workflow can vary between agents, brokers, developers and agencies, but the goal remains the same: keep customer information and sales activity organized throughout the process.

BROSAVO focuses on this real estate-specific workflow.

## How Does a Real Estate CRM App Work?

A real estate CRM app typically centralizes information that would otherwise be managed through several different tools.

A simplified workflow looks like this:

### 1. Lead

A potential customer enters the sales process.

The lead becomes a record that the sales team can manage rather than an enquiry that exists only in a message, spreadsheet or notebook.

### 2. Qualification

The agent or sales team understands the customer's requirements.

For example, the team may need to understand:

- Property type
- Location preference
- Budget
- Purchase requirements
- Timeline
- Other relevant requirements

### 3. Property Requirement

The customer's requirement can be connected with relevant property information.

This helps the sales team focus on suitable opportunities instead of treating every enquiry the same way.

### 4. Follow-Up

The sales team can keep track of follow-up activities and continue the conversation with the customer.

This is especially important in real estate because property purchases often involve multiple interactions before a decision is made.

### 5. Site Visit

When applicable to the business workflow, a site visit can become another step in the sales process.

### 6. Negotiation

Once a customer shows serious interest, the opportunity can progress toward negotiation and the next sales stage.

### 7. Closing

The final objective is to convert the opportunity into a successful property transaction.

A CRM helps provide visibility throughout this journey.
