# Real Estate CRM Canada: A Modern CRM for Canadian Realtors

Managing leads, clients, follow-ups, properties and conversations can become difficult when everything is spread across spreadsheets, email, messaging apps and multiple disconnected tools.

Brosavo is a modern **real estate CRM for Canadian realtors, agents, teams and brokerages** designed to bring those workflows together in one organized platform.

Whether you are an independent REALTOR®, a growing real estate team or a brokerage managing multiple agents, a dedicated CRM can help you create a more consistent process for capturing leads, following up with prospects and managing client relationships.

## What Is a Real Estate CRM?

A real estate CRM, or customer relationship management system, is software designed to help real estate professionals manage relationships with current and potential clients.

CREA describes CRM systems as tools that can help real estate professionals manage client interactions, track leads, marketing campaigns and other actionable business information.

For a real estate professional, this can mean having one place to manage:

- Leads and prospects
- Buyers and sellers
- Client contact information
- Follow-up activities
- Property interests
- Sales pipelines
- Notes and conversations
- Team assignments
- Marketing enquiries
- Tasks and reminders

Instead of relying on spreadsheets, notebooks and multiple communication tools, a CRM gives your team a structured view of the customer journey.

## Why Canadian Realtors Need a Real Estate CRM

Canadian real estate professionals receive enquiries from many different sources.

A potential client might come through:

- A brokerage website
- A personal real estate website
- REALTOR.ca-related channels
- Social media
- Google search
- Online advertising
- Referrals
- Open houses
- Property enquiries
- Direct phone calls
- Email
- Messaging platforms

When these enquiries are managed separately, it becomes easier for leads to be forgotten or follow-ups to be delayed.

A real estate CRM can bring these interactions into a centralized workflow.

### Capture Leads in One Place

Every new enquiry should have a clear destination.

Instead of manually copying information between spreadsheets and applications, your CRM can provide a central location for organizing prospects and assigning follow-up actions.

A typical lead profile can contain:

- Name
- Phone number
- Email
- City or location
- Buyer or seller status
- Property interests
- Budget
- Lead source
- Assigned agent
- Notes
- Follow-up history

This gives agents a clearer picture of every opportunity.

## Follow Up Without Losing Track of Leads

Real estate is highly relationship-driven.

A lead that is not ready to buy today may become a serious buyer or seller several weeks or months later.

A CRM can help agents organize follow-up activities so important conversations do not disappear inside an inbox.

For example:

**New Lead → Contacted → Qualified → Showing → Offer → Closed**

Your team can create its own pipeline stages based on its workflow.

The goal is simple: every opportunity should have a clear next step.

## Manage Clients and Leads Together

A real estate CRM should not simply be a contact database.

It should help you understand the relationship behind each contact.

For example:

**Sarah Mitchell**

Toronto, Ontario

Buyer

Budget: $850,000

Preferred areas: North York, Etobicoke

Status: Qualified

Next follow-up: Friday

Notes: Looking for a 3-bedroom home close to transit.

When information like this is available in one place, an agent can have a much more informed conversation with the client.
