## What Is a Real Estate CRM for Brokers?

A **real estate CRM for brokers** is software that helps brokerage businesses manage leads, agents, customers, property-related opportunities, follow-ups, and sales pipelines from a centralized system.

Unlike a CRM used only by an individual agent, a brokerage CRM needs to provide visibility across the wider sales operation.

A broker may need to know:

- How many leads are coming into the business
- Which agents are handling those leads
- Which opportunities are active
- Which prospects require follow-up
- Where opportunities are in the sales pipeline
- Which properties are being discussed
- How the brokerage's sales activity is progressing

Brosavo is positioned as a Real Estate CRM for businesses that need to organize these property-focused sales workflows.

## Why Do Real Estate Brokerages Need CRM Software?

A brokerage can have multiple agents handling many enquiries at the same time.

Without a centralized system, lead information can become fragmented across spreadsheets, messaging applications, notes, emails, and individual agent records.

This creates several operational problems.

### Common brokerage challenges include:

- Managing leads across multiple agents
- Assigning new enquiries
- Monitoring follow-ups
- Tracking active opportunities
- Maintaining customer information
- Managing property enquiries
- Understanding pipeline activity
- Measuring team activity
- Preventing leads from being forgotten
- Understanding where sales opportunities are progressing or slowing down

A brokerage CRM gives the business a centralized framework for managing these activities.

The goal is not simply to store customer information.

The goal is to give the brokerage better visibility into its sales operation.
