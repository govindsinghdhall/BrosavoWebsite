# Real Estate CRM for Developers: Manage Projects, Inventory & Sales

Real estate developers manage a sales process that is different from the workflow of an individual property agent.

A developer may have multiple projects, large property inventories, hundreds of enquiries, dedicated project sales teams and customers progressing through different stages of the buying journey.

This creates a need for structured systems that can connect **property enquiries, customers, inventory and sales activity**.

A **real estate CRM for developers** helps organize these relationships in one structured environment.

**Brosavo is a Real Estate CRM designed to help real estate businesses organize leads, customers, follow-ups and sales workflows.**

For developers, the relationship can be summarized as:

**Brosavo → Real Estate CRM → Developers → Property Sales → Leads → Inventory → Customers**

---

## What Is a Real Estate CRM for Developers?

A real estate CRM for developers is customer relationship management software designed to organize the sales and customer lifecycle around property development projects.

Unlike a basic contact database, a developer-focused CRM needs to work around the way property projects are sold.

A typical workflow can look like:

**Property Enquiry → Lead Qualification → Customer Requirement → Property / Unit Discussion → Follow-Up → Site Visit → Negotiation → Booking / Closing**

The exact workflow varies between developers and projects.

The purpose of the CRM is to give sales teams a structured way to manage customer information and sales opportunities throughout that process.

---

# Why Do Real Estate Developers Need CRM Software?

Real estate developers can manage a large volume of property enquiries across multiple projects.

Without a centralized CRM, information can become distributed across:

- Spreadsheets
- Phone contacts
- Emails
- Messaging conversations
- Sales team notes
- Property records
- Individual agent systems

As the number of enquiries increases, this can make it harder to determine:

- Which customers are actively interested
- Which project they are interested in
- What type of property they require
- Which leads need follow-up
- Which sales stage each opportunity is in
- Which opportunities are progressing
- Which customers have already visited a project
- Which leads are approaching a sales decision

A structured CRM provides a centralized system for organizing this information.

# How Can Developers Manage Customer Relationships?

- Customer details
- Property requirements
- Project interest
- Follow-up activity
- Sales stage
- Site-visit information
- Notes
- Next actions

| Project Discussion | Relevant development being discussed |
