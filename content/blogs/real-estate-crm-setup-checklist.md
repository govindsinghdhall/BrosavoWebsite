# Real Estate CRM Setup Checklist for a New Agency

Setting up a real estate CRM is more than just installing software – it's about transforming how your agency operates. The success of your implementation depends not only on choosing the right platform but also on proper configuration, team alignment, and systematic execution.

Whether you're a new agency starting from scratch or an established business looking to upgrade, following a comprehensive setup checklist ensures that you build a system that works for your specific needs rather than trying to force your workflows into a generic template.
