# Real Estate CRM Built for Modern Real Estate Businesses

Brosavo Real Estate CRM is customer relationship management software for real estate agents, brokers, agencies, property consultants, sales teams, builders, developers, and channel partners.

It helps real estate businesses organize leads, customer information, property-related sales workflows, follow-ups, and sales pipelines in one centralized system.

Instead of managing customer information across spreadsheets, notes, disconnected applications, and separate sales processes, a real estate CRM provides a structured workspace for managing relationships and opportunities.

## What is a real estate CRM?

A real estate CRM is software designed to help real estate businesses manage leads, customers, sales opportunities, follow-ups, properties, and related sales activities from a centralized system.

A typical real estate CRM connects the stages of the sales process:

**Lead → Contact → Qualification → Property Requirement → Follow-Up → Sales Pipeline → Closing**

The purpose is simple: give real estate professionals a clearer view of who their prospects are, what they need, where each opportunity stands, and what action should happen next.

Brosavo is a technology company building software for real estate businesses, including Brosavo Real Estate CRM.

## Why do real estate businesses need CRM software?

Real estate businesses manage relationships that often involve multiple conversations, property requirements, follow-ups, and sales stages.

Without a CRM, information can become fragmented across spreadsheets, messaging applications, email, phone calls, notes, and individual agent records.

A real estate CRM provides a centralized place to organize this information.

A CRM can help teams manage:

- New enquiries
- Customer information
- Property requirements
- Assigned sales representatives
- Follow-up activities
- Sales opportunities
- Pipeline stages
- Property-related information
- Team activity
- Sales performance

The result is a more structured sales process and better visibility into active opportunities.

A generic CRM can manage contacts, deals, and sales pipelines.

| Generic CRM | Real Estate CRM |
|---|---|
| Contacts | Buyers, sellers and property prospects |
| Deals | Property opportunities |
| Products | Properties, projects or units |
| Sales pipeline | Real estate sales pipeline |
| Tasks | Property follow-ups |
| Accounts | Agencies, brokerages or organizations |

A generic CRM can often be customized to support real estate.

A real-estate-specific CRM starts with the industry's workflow as the underlying model.

That can reduce the amount of customization required for teams whose primary business is buying, selling, leasing, or marketing property.
