SEO, AEO and GEO are three layers of the same problem: **can the right customer find you, trust you, and enquire?**

Brosavo is a software, technology and digital marketing company. We build the website first, then implement search as architecture — not as an afterthought PDF.

This guide explains the difference, why it matters for Google Ads landing pages, and how we ship it on [website development](/website-development), [SEO](/seo), [AEO](/aeo) and [GEO](/geo) programmes.

## What SEO still does

Search engine optimization is how you earn visibility in **ranked lists**: Google organic results, local pack, and similar engines.

A useful SEO programme includes:

- Technical access: indexation, canonicals, sitemaps, Core Web Vitals
- Intent mapping: service, location, comparison and guide pages
- On-page structure: titles, headings, internal links
- Local SEO when you actually serve a city
- Measurement in Search Console and analytics tied to **leads**, not vanity sessions

If the site cannot be crawled or is too slow, ads Quality Score and organic rankings both suffer. That is why Brosavo treats [technical SEO](/technical-seo) as engineering work.

## What AEO adds

Answer Engine Optimization prepares content for **questions**, not only keywords.

People now get answers from:

- Google AI Overviews
- ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity
- Voice assistants
- In-product copilots

AEO work looks like:

- A question inventory from sales calls, People Also Ask, and ads search terms
- Short, extractable answers near the top of the relevant page
- FAQ and HowTo schema that matches visible copy
- Organization, service and place entities so models know who you are

AEO is why every Brosavo commercial hub has FAQ schema and why city pages answer “do you serve this market?” directly. See [AEO services](/aeo).

## What GEO adds

Generative Engine Optimization is about being used as a **source** when an AI system writes a paragraph, a shopping shortlist, or a local recommendation.

GEO cares about:

- Original, specific, first-party detail (not spun blogs)
- Clean structure: headings, tables, FAQs, consistent entities
- Corroboration: internal links, proof, current dates
- Freshness for pricing, inventory and definitions

GEO does not replace SEO. A generative system still needs a page it can fetch and trust. See [GEO services](/geo).

## How this changes website development

A brochure site with a contact form is not enough for 2026 acquisition.

The website should include:

1. **Commercial hubs** — website development, SEO, AEO, GEO, digital marketing
2. **Country and city pages** for markets you truly serve (India, Canada, US, UK, Australia)
3. **Supporting pages** — redesign, landing pages, ecommerce, local SEO, CRO, maintenance
4. **Guides** that internal-link into those money pages
5. **Conversion** — forms, WhatsApp, CRM routing, ads tracking

That is the same pattern we use for [real estate website development](/real-estate-website-development) and [Real Estate CRM location pages](/real-estate-crm).

## Google Ads and organic should share one conversion layer

Paid search should not dump traffic on a slow homepage.

Use:

- [Landing page development](/landing-page-development) for message-matched ads
- [Google Ads landing systems](/google-ads) for tracking and Quality Score
- Indexable city and service pages for the queries you want to own after you stop bidding

Winning ads copy should inform SEO titles and AEO answers. That is how cost per lead falls over time.
